Our Evolution: Form & Function

Vosme International Sdn Bhd was established in 2014 as a specialized bespoke software house. Over more than a decade, we have watched the web grow and change. When cognitive machine learning and large neural networks emerged, we leveraged our deep systems-integration expertise to transition into one of the region's leading AI consultation and workflow automation agencies. Today, we bridge the gap between classical cloud environments and cutting-edge artificial intelligence models.
